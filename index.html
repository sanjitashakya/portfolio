About Me

Hi! I'm Sanjita, a Bachelor in Computer Applications (BCA) graduate from Bhaktapur who enjoys both sides of tech work: building the product and managing the work that gets it delivered.

On the development side, I build web apps with React, Next.js, Django and PHP. On the management side, I've run Agile sprints with Jira and Asana at Cloco, led a small Scrum team on the Splitfair app, and coordinated daily operations, staff and reporting across multiple outlets at Jammy's.

Today I bring the two together as Website & Digital Operations Coordinator at Nepal Connect, where I manage the website, audit and improve it, and coordinate content and digital initiatives.
